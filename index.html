ADU as a Rental: What Landlords Actually Need to Plan For

Homeowner guide · 5 min read

Building an ADU specifically to rent out changes several design and planning decisions compared to building one for family use. A few of these are easy to overlook until well after the unit is already built.

Separate utility metering pays for itself

Without separate metering, a landlord absorbs a tenant's utility usage as part of the rent, with no easy way to pass through actual consumption. Separate metering costs more to install upfront but gives a much cleaner ongoing arrangement and avoids disputes over usage down the line.

Independent access matters more than it seems

A shared driveway or a path that crosses close to the main house's windows can create friction with tenants and reduce the unit's rental appeal compared to one with a clearly independent entrance and parking. This is worth prioritizing in site planning even if it means a slightly less convenient layout in other respects.

Soundproofing is a real, ongoing consideration

Tenant noise reaching the main house — or vice versa — is one of the more common landlord-tenant friction points with an ADU on the same property. Additional insulation and sound-dampening in shared walls or nearby structures is a relatively small added cost during construction that meaningfully reduces this risk later.

Local rental regulations may add requirements beyond zoning

Some jurisdictions layer additional rental-specific requirements onto an ADU beyond the base zoning approval — minimum owner-occupancy of the main house, separate rental licensing, or specific safety features. Checking these rental-specific rules is a separate step from confirming the ADU is zoning-approved to be built at all.

Getting the underlying approval right comes first

None of the rental-specific planning matters if the ADU isn't approvable on the lot to begin with. a rundown of the zoning and utility questions that determine ADU approval in Colorado Springs covers that foundational step in more detail.
